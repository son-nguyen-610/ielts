# #22 · Task 1: Smartphone ownership

*The graphs below give information concerning smartphone ownership as a percentage of population in a country from 2000 to 2010, and by level of education for the years 2000 and 2010. Summarise the data by selecting and reporting the main features and make comparisons where relevant.*

![Task 1 chart](https://engnovatewebsitestorage.blob.core.windows.net/ielts-writing-task-1-images/88d0e76dc70b8200)

Chia nhóm: biểu đồ 1 / biểu đồ 2. Ý đáng nêu ở overview là khoảng cách giữa các trình độ học vấn thu hẹp lại, không chỉ là mọi nhóm đều tăng.

**Model answer — 196 words**

*The two charts show smartphone ownership as a percentage of the population between 2000 and 2010, together with ownership by level of education in 2000 and 2010.*

*Overall, smartphone ownership rose substantially across the population during the decade. It is also clear that ownership increased at every educational level, with the largest gains occurring among those with the least formal education.*

*Overall ownership stood at 41% in 2000 and climbed throughout the period. Growth was modest until 2004, when the figure reached 52%, but accelerated noticeably thereafter. Ownership then rose to 74% in 2008 before reaching 85% by the end of the decade.*

*Turning to education, ownership increased with each additional qualification in both years. In 2000, only 15% of those without a high school certificate owned a smartphone, compared with 76% of those holding a doctorate. By 2010 this gap had narrowed considerably, as the former group reached 45% while the latter reached 90%. The sharpest rise occurred among high school graduates, whose ownership almost doubled from 32% to 61%.*
