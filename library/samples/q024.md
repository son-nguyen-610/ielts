# Task 1 · Sample 13: Reasons for study and employer support

*The charts below show the main reasons for study among students of different age groups and the amount of support they received from employers. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features and make comparisons where relevant.*

![Chart](img/w01-task1.png)

Grouping: chart 1 by motive, career falling and interest rising with the crossover at 40 to 49. Chart 2 on its own, a U shape with its low point at 30 to 39. Overview carries no numbers.

**Model answer**

*The first chart shows the main reasons people of different ages gave for studying, while the second shows how much support students in each age group received from their employers.*

*Overall, career was the dominant motive among the youngest students and interest among the oldest, with the two reasons balanced in the middle of the range. Employer support was highest for the youngest group and lowest for students in their thirties.*

*Among students under 26 career was by far the more common reason, at 80 percent against only 10 percent for interest. This gap narrowed steadily across the next two groups, falling to 70 against 15 percent among those aged 26 to 29 and 58 against 25 percent among those aged 30 to 39. The two motives met at 40 percent each in the 40 to 49 group. Among students over 49 the pattern had reversed completely, with interest reaching 70 percent and career falling to 20.*

*Employer support followed a different shape, beginning at 64 percent for students under 26 and declining to a low of 34 percent among those aged 30 to 39. From that point it recovered, rising to 38 percent at 40 to 49 and 44 percent among students over 49. Even at its highest among older learners, support never returned to the level offered to the youngest group.*
