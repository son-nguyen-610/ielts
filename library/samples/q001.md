# #1 · Task 1: University library users

*The chart and table below show the results of a survey of library users at a university. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.*

Grouping: chart 1 / chart 2. Inside the table, split the two aspects nobody criticised from the three resource aspects.

**Model answer — 197 words**

*The pie chart shows how library users at a university divide into five categories, while the table gives their satisfaction with five aspects of the service.*

*Overall, full-time students accounted for the great majority of users, while academic staff formed by far the smallest group. It is also clear that the staff drew the most positive response of any aspect, whereas the journals drew the least.*

*Full-time undergraduates were the largest category, at 44%. Full-time postgraduates accounted for exactly a quarter, while part-time postgraduates made up a further 16%, meaning that postgraduates together represented 41% of all users. The two remaining groups were considerably smaller, with distance learners at 8% and academic staff at just 7%.*

*Turning to satisfaction, opening hours and the helpfulness of staff were the only aspects with which nobody was dissatisfied, and 95% rated the staff very satisfactory, the highest figure in the table. The three resource-related aspects all attracted some criticism. Half of the users were very satisfied with the availability of books, and wi-fi reliability received a similar response, at 48%. Journals were rated worst, with both the lowest approval, at 45%, and the highest dissatisfaction, at 20%.*
