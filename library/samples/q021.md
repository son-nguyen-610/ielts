**Model answer — 188 words**

*The bar chart compares the share of global car sharing usage held by five regions in 2008 and 2016.*

*Overall, North America and Europe dominated car sharing in both years, although their positions reversed over the period. It is also clear that the three remaining regions held only marginal shares throughout.*

*In 2008, Europe accounted for the largest share by a considerable margin, at 59%. North America followed with 32%, meaning that these two regions together represented over 90% of global usage. By 2016 the relationship had reversed, as the European figure fell to 34% while North America rose to 51%.*

*The remaining three regions changed very little. Asia held the largest of these shares in both years, rising from 4% to 6%. Australia increased from 3% to 5%, and South America from 2% to 4%. Taken together, these regions still accounted for under a sixth of global usage at the end of the period, despite each one growing.*
