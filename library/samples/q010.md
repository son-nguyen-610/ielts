# #10 · Task 1: Charlestown weight assessment

*The charts below show the weight assessment of inhabitants of Charlestown in 1955 and 2015. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.*

![Task 1 chart](https://ieltspracticeonline.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/WRITING-TASK-1-the-weight-measurements-of-people-living-in-Charlestown-in-1955-and-2015.png)

Grouping: 1955 / 2015. Do not describe ten bars one by one. The answer is the reversal between ideal weight and excess weight, and the fact that obesity rises with age only in 2015.

**Model answer — 201 words**

*The two charts compare the weight distribution of residents of Charlestown across five age groups in 1955 and in 2015.*

*Overall, the proportion of residents carrying excess weight rose dramatically over the sixty-year period, while the ideal-weight and underweight categories declined. It is also clear that obesity increased with age in 2015, whereas in 1955 the oldest group contained the highest proportion of underweight residents.*

*In 1955, the majority of residents in every age group were of ideal weight, with figures ranging from roughly 60% to 70%. Underweight residents formed the second largest category throughout, accounting for approximately a quarter of those aged 20 to 29 and around 40% of those aged 60 to 69. Obesity was negligible, remaining below 5% in all five groups.*

*By 2015, this pattern had reversed. The proportion classified as obese rose steadily with age, from under 10% among residents in their twenties to roughly 58% among those aged 60 to 69. Overweight residents accounted for a further quarter to third of the older groups, which left ideal weight as a minority category beyond the age of 40. Underweight residents had almost disappeared from every group except the youngest.*
