**Model answer — 196 words**

*The diagram illustrates the main components of a hot air balloon and the principle that enables it to rise and descend.*

*Overall, the balloon consists of three sections, the fabric envelope, the burner assembly and the basket. It is also clear that its movement depends entirely on the difference in weight between heated and unheated air.*

*The envelope is constructed from vertical strips called panels, which are grouped into larger sections known as gores. At the very top sits the parachute valve, a self-sealing flap through which hot air can be released at a controlled rate. The lower part of the envelope narrows into a skirt, which is connected by ropes to a basket. A burner and a propane cylinder are mounted directly above the basket.*

*The balloon operates by heating the air held inside the envelope. Propane from the cylinder is mixed with air and ignited by the burner, which produces a flame directed upwards. Because heated air is lighter than the cool air surrounding it, it rises and carries the balloon with it. When the parachute valve is opened, hot air escapes gradually and the balloon descends.*
