**Model answer**

*The pie charts compare the age structure of the populations of Yemen and Italy in 2000 with projections for 2050.*

*Overall, both populations are expected to grow older across the fifty year period. The shift is projected to be far more pronounced in Italy, where the oldest group is set to become the largest.*

*In 2000 Yemen had a very young population, with children under 15 making up just over half of the total. Those aged 15 to 59 accounted for 46.3 percent and only 3.6 percent of people were aged 60 or over. By 2050 the share of children is projected to fall to 37 percent and the working age group to rise to 57.3 percent, with the oldest group still below 6 percent.*

*Italy's profile in 2000 was close to the reverse. Children represented 14.3 percent of the population and those over 60 a further 24.1 percent, leaving 61.6 percent of working age. By 2050 the working age share is projected to drop sharply to 46.2 percent. Meanwhile the over sixties are expected to reach 42.3 percent, nearly four times the share held by children.*
