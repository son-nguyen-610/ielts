## Task 2 — 16 câu

A = phe mình bênh. B = phe còn lại.

**Mở bài**
1. It is often claimed that **A**.
2. Others, however, insist that **B**.
3. In my view, **A** deserves priority, because ___.

**Thân 1 — bênh A**
4. *Luận điểm* — The strongest argument for **A** is ___.
5. *Thực trạng* — At present, ___.
6. *Cơ chế* — Once ___, ___.
7. *Dẫn chứng* — ___ illustrates this: ___.

**Thân 2 — khen B**
8. Admittedly, **B** brings real benefits.
9. For many ___, ___.
10. Without ___, ___.
11. ___ shows this clearly: ___.

**Thân 3 — lật**
12. Even so, **B** is barely achievable without **A**.
13. **B** requires ___, which ___ cannot provide alone.
14. This is why ___.

**Kết bài**
15. In conclusion, although **B** matters, **A** must come first, because ___.
16. The better approach is not to choose, but to ___.

Câu 3, 15, 16 phải cùng một phe. Câu 13 nhắc lại đúng cái khó vừa nêu ở câu 10.

*It is often claimed that economic growth should be a government's first priority. Others, however, insist that protecting the environment matters more. In my view, growth deserves priority, because it funds the very protection its critics demand.*

*It is often claimed that economic growth should be a government's primary objective. Others, however, insist that environmental protection is more important. In my view, growth deserves priority, because it funds the protection that its critics demand.*

*The strongest argument for growth is its capacity to reduce poverty. At present, a significant proportion of households in developing countries cannot afford adequate housing, nutrition or medical care. Once an economy expands, employment and wages rise, which enables these households to meet basic needs and to contribute through taxation. For example, a worker who moves from subsistence farming into waged employment can afford schooling that was previously impossible.*

*Admittedly, environmental protection brings substantial benefits. For many cities, industrial and vehicle emissions have raised pollution to levels that damage respiratory health. Without regulatory intervention, this damage accumulates faster than it can be reversed. For example, one factory discharging untreated waste into a river can destroy the fishing that every household downstream depends on.*

*Even so, environmental protection is barely achievable without a strong economy. Renewable energy, waste treatment and emissions monitoring require sustained public investment, which low-income states cannot provide. This is why wealthier economies regulate pollution more effectively than poorer ones, regardless of political intention.*

*In conclusion, the economic benefits of growth outweigh the environmental costs attributed to it, because growth supplies the resources that protection requires. The better approach is not to choose between the two objectives but to fund environmental safeguards from the revenue that growth generates.*

## Task 1 — 7 câu
1. The ___ shows ___ in ___ between ___ and ___.
2. Overall, ___. *(cấm số)*
3. It is also clear that ___, while ___. *(cấm số)*
4. *Nhóm 1, mục chính* — ___ was the largest, at ___%.
5. *Nhóm 1, mục thứ hai* — ___ accounted for ___%, while ___ made up ___%.
6. *Nhóm 2, mục chính* — ___ was much smaller, at ___%.
7. *Số cuối hoặc ngoại lệ* — ___ was the smallest, at just ___%.

Chia nhóm: mục tăng / mục giảm · mục lớn / mục nhỏ · nửa đầu / nửa sau quy trình · cái bị bỏ / cái được thêm (bản đồ).

**Làm mẫu — pie chart**

*The pie chart shows how library users at a university divide into five categories. Overall, full-time students account for the great majority of users. It is also clear that undergraduates form the single largest group, while academic staff are by far the smallest.*

*Full-time undergraduates represent the largest category, at 44%. Full-time postgraduates account for exactly a quarter, while part-time postgraduates make up a further 16%.*

*The two remaining groups are considerably smaller. Distance learners constitute 8%, and academic staff just 7%.*

**Làm mẫu — quy trình**

*The diagram illustrates how instant coffee is produced, from harvesting to packaging. Overall, the process involves seven stages and is almost entirely mechanised. It is also clear that the beans are treated twice by heat, once before grinding and once after.*

*The process begins when ripe cherries are picked and the beans are separated from the pulp. The beans are then dried in the sun for several days, after which they are roasted at high temperature.*

*Once roasted, the beans are ground and brewed into a concentrated liquid. In the final stage, this liquid is freeze-dried into granules and sealed into jars.*

## Speaking — 3 câu

2. ___, mainly because ___.

Gặp câu không biết gì:

*I don't know it as well as I'd like to. The only time I see it is ___. I'd enjoy it more if ___.*
