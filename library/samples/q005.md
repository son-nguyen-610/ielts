# #5 · Task 1: Production of a lead pencil

*The diagram below shows the production of a lead pencil. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.*

Grouping: forestry stages / manufacturing stages. The timing labels stop once the trees are felled, which is what marks the split. Present simple passive throughout, and every stage must appear.

**Model answer — 191 words**

*The diagram illustrates the stages involved in manufacturing a lead pencil, from the planting of seeds to the finished product.*

*Overall, the process consists of two distinct phases, a lengthy period of forestry lasting more than seventeen years and a far shorter industrial phase. It is also clear that almost every stage after the timber is felled is mechanised.*

*The process begins when seeds are planted and left to germinate for four months. The young trees are then replanted and grown for a further three years, after which the plantation is thinned so that the remaining trees have more space. These trees are left to mature for a further fourteen years before they are felled and cut into logs.*

*In the manufacturing phase, the logs are sliced into thin slats, which are treated and stored for sixty days. Grooves are then cut into each slat and lengths of black lead are glued into place. A second slat is glued over the first to form a sandwich, which is hard-pressed under heat. Finally, the pressed block is cut into individual pencils, which are painted, sharpened and stamped before distribution.*
