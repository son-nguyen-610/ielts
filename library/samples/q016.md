# Task 1 · Sample 9: Health centre ratings

*The table below shows the results of a survey of patients at three health centres. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.*

![Task 1 chart](https://engnovatewebsitestorage.blob.core.windows.net/ielts-writing-task-1-images/e4519b2eaba9a254)

Chia nhóm: trung tâm điểm cao (Peveril, Marchbank) / trung tâm điểm thấp (Longston). Ý đáng nêu ở overview là khoảng cách giữa ba nơi rất lớn ở mảng hành chính nhưng gần như bằng nhau ở mảng chuyên môn.

**Model answer — 192 words**

*The table compares user ratings for five aspects of service at three health centres, on a scale from one to ten.*

*Overall, Peveril Centre was rated considerably higher than the other two centres, while Longston Centre received the lowest score for every aspect. It is also clear that the gap between the centres was far wider for administrative matters than for medical care.*

*Peveril Centre achieved the highest overall average, at 8.3, and led in all five categories. Its strongest results were for responding to concerns and booking appointments, at 9.6 and 9.1 respectively. Marchbank Centre followed with an overall average of 7.2, performing well on booking appointments at 7.8 but considerably less well on responses to concerns at 6.5.*

*Longston Centre was rated lowest overall, at 5.8. Its weakest area was its response to concerns, which received 4.3, the lowest figure in the table and less than half the equivalent score at Peveril. Doctors' service was the one aspect on which the three centres were broadly comparable, with Longston scoring 8 against 8.7 and 8.4 elsewhere. Pharmacy services were rated poorly at all three centres, with no score above 6.3.*
