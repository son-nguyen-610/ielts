IELTS Speaking Test

Fifteen minutes, three parts, one take. The questions are read out to you and the clock runs on its own. Nothing can be paused, replayed or re-recorded.

Leave this on. Turn it off only if the microphone meter stays flat while you speak.

Rules

  • Use Chrome on a laptop with headphones. Phones and Safari will not record properly.
  • Each question is read once. You get one repeat for the whole test.
  • When the clock reaches zero the next question starts, even mid-sentence.
  • In Part 2 you get one minute to make notes, then two minutes to speak.
  • Leaving this tab is recorded in the report. Do not open a dictionary or another window.
  • At the end you download four files and upload them to the homework form.
Stay on this page until the download buttons appear. If you close the tab early, the recording is lost and the test counts as not submitted.
