# #3 · Task 1: UK and Ireland independent films

*The table below gives information about UK independent films. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.*

Grouping: genres that released many films but earned little / genres that released few but earned much. That mismatch is the whole answer — do not read the table row by row.

**Model answer — 191 words**

*The table shows how many independent films were released in the UK and Republic of Ireland in 2012 by genre, together with each genre's share of total releases and of box-office income.*

*Overall, the genres that produced the most films were not the genres that earned the most money. It is also clear that comedy dominated income while drama and documentary dominated output.*

*Drama and documentary were the joint largest categories, with 35 releases each, together accounting for almost half of the 148 films released. However, their commercial returns were slight, as drama took only 8.3% of ticket income and documentary just 2.9%, the lowest figure of any substantial genre.*

*Comedy showed the opposite pattern. Although it produced 26 films, or 17.6% of releases, it generated 45.4% of all income, more than the next two genres combined. Horror followed the same shape on a smaller scale, earning 20.2% of income from 9.5% of releases, and a single biopic, 0.7% of output, returned 9.1%.*

*The remaining genres were less impressive**. Crime was the only genre whose shares matched exactly, at 4.7%, while thriller and romance earned 1.3% and 0.8% respectively.*
