# Speaking

Mọi câu trả lời: **trả lời thẳng → lý do → chi tiết thật**. Part 2 là 3 câu đó kéo dài.

Giám khảo chấm trôi chảy, từ vựng, ngữ pháp, phát âm — không chấm ý hay dở

Nói sai thì nói tiếp. Chỉ sửa ngay lúc vừa sai: *Sorry, I mean …*

Im quá 3 giây mất nhiều hơn một lỗi ngữ pháp.

Không nhớ từ thì tả vòng: *It's like a … / a kind of …*

## Luật câu

Nội dung giống nhau, đặt câu khác nhau là chênh cả band.

**Mỗi câu trả lời chỉ 1 câu được mở đầu bằng* I***.**

*I like it because I can relax.* → *What I like about it is how relaxing it is.*

Đổi chủ ngữ bằng: *What I like about X is … / There's something about X that … / People my age tend to … / It's the kind of thing that …*

**Mỗi câu trả lời ít nhất 1 mệnh đề phụ** — *which, because, when, if, even though*.

*It's cheap. I go there often.* → *It's cheap enough that I end up going there most weekends.*

**So sánh hay miêu tả phải dùng đúng tính từ**.

*Hanoi is better.* → *Hanoi is far livelier, though nowhere near as green.*

**Part 3 phải có 1 câu điều kiện.**

*The government should help.* → *If the government subsidised it, far more people would switch.*

## Bộ khung theo dạng câu hỏi

Nghe câu hỏi, nhận ra dạng, chạy đúng 3 câu.

**Thích / không thích** — *Do you like cooking?*
1. Trả lời thẳng, kèm giới hạn
2. Lý do chính
3. Chi tiết thật, gần đây

*Yes, though mainly at weekends, when I'm not rushing. What I enjoy is the part where the kitchen smells of garlic and nobody is talking about work. Last Sunday I spent nearly two hours on a pot of bún bò, which is ridiculous for one meal, but that was rather the point.*

**So sánh** — *Is it better to live in a city or the countryside?*
1. Chốt luôn bên nào hơn
2. Nói bên thứ nhất
3. Nói bên thứ hai

*I'd say a city suits me far better at this stage of my life. Cities give you work and things to do at midnight, which is what I need while I'm still building a career. The countryside is calmer and much cheaper, but it's the kind of place I'd move to later, not now.*

**Không biết, chưa từng gặp** — *Do you enjoy classical music?*
1. Thừa nhận ít tiếp xúc
2. Chỗ mình từng thấy nó
3. Điều kiện để mình thích nó hơn

*Honestly, I don't know it as well as I'd like to. The only time I really hear it is in coffee shops, or sometimes in film soundtracks. I think I'd enjoy it a lot more if someone explained what to listen for.*

**Tần suất, thói quen** — *How often do you use public transport?*
1. Tần suất + khi nào
2. Vì sao chừng đó
3. Lần gần nhất

*Almost every weekday, usually twice a day. It's mainly because parking near my office costs more than the fare does, so there's no real argument for driving. This morning I took the bus in and got through a whole chapter of my book on the way.*

**Nguyên nhân** — *Why do people …?*
1. Lý do chính
2. Cơ chế: vì sao dẫn đến
3. Ví dụ hoặc so sánh

*I'd say the main reason is that nobody knows how it ends. Films and novels are written in advance, whereas a match can turn in the final minute, which is what keeps people watching something they could easily switch off. That's probably why a dull game with one*

*sudden goal stays in the memory longer than a well-played one that finished exactly as expected.*

**Quan điểm** — *Should governments pay for public transport?*
1. Chốt quan điểm
2. Lý do + cơ chế
3. Nhượng bộ ngắn: *That said, …*

*I'd say they should, particularly in developing countries. Most families there can't afford a cleaner option on their own, so if fares stay low, people will actually leave their motorbikes at home. That said, it only works if the buses are frequent enough to rely on.*

**Dự đoán** — *Will … in the future?*
1. Dự đoán + mức chắc chắn
2. Vì sao
3. Điều kiện ngược: *unless …*

*Probably fewer, though I doubt printed books will disappear altogether. Screens are simply more convenient for most reading, and students my age already treat paper as the backup rather than the default. Unless publishers make printed editions feel like objects worth owning, that decline will carry on quietly for years.*

## Part 2 — 1 phút chuẩn bị, nói 2 phút

Viết 4 gạch đầu dòng theo 4 dòng trên thẻ, mỗi gạch 2–3 chữ. Cấm viết thành câu — viết câu xong là đọc, mà đọc thì nghe ra ngay.
1. *I'd like to talk about ___, which ___.*
2. *I came across it ___, when I was ___.* — khi nào, ở đâu, với ai
3. Kể chuyện, 3 câu, thì quá khứ, thêm 1 chi tiết giác quan
4. *What made it special was ___.*
5. *That's why I still ___ it.*

Hết ý trước 2 phút thì kéo theo thứ tự: so sánh (*Compared to ___, this one was …*) → đổi mốc thời gian (hồi đó so với bây giờ) → nói ảnh hưởng (*Because of that, I started …*).

Giám khảo cắt lời là tốt, nghĩa là bạn nói đủ.

## Cụm phải thuộc

Câu giờ nửa giây — *Well, … / I suppose … / To be honest, …*

Nêu ý kiến — *I'd say … / From what I've seen, … / Personally, …*

Mức độ chắc chắn — *definitely / probably / it's hard to say*

Nêu lý do — *mainly because … / the main reason is …*

Nêu ví dụ — *For instance, … / Take ___, for example.*

Sửa lỗi vừa nói — *Sorry, I mean …*

Nhượng bộ — *That said, … / Although in some cases …*

Không hiểu câu hỏi — *Sorry, do you mean ___?*

Cấm dùng: *In my opinion, I think…* · *As we all know…* · *Firstly, secondly, thirdly* trong Part 2.

## 4 lỗi mất điểm nhất

*I like book* → *I like books*. Âm /s/, /z/, /t/, /d/ phải bật ra.

*Yesterday I go there* → *Yesterday I went there*. Part 2 gần như luôn là quá khứ.

*Although it's hard, but I enjoy it* → bỏ *but*.

*I work in office* → *I work in an office*.

Trọng âm từ quan trọng hơn phát âm từng âm. Sai trọng âm (*comFORtable*) thì người nghe không ra chữ; phát âm hơi lệch mà đúng trọng âm thì vẫn hiểu.
