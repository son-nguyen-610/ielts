# Task 2 · Sample 15: Financial aid or practical help

*Some people think rich countries should give financial aid to poorer ones. Others argue that practical help is more useful. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.*

Dạng bàn hai phía + ý kiến. Bộ xương gốc, không đổi gì. Thân 3 dùng Feed, tiền là điều kiện để có hỗ trợ thực tế.

**Model answer**

*Opinion is divided over whether rich countries should assist poorer ones with money or with practical support. Both forms of help are defended on reasonable grounds. In my opinion practical assistance produces the more durable result, although money is often needed to begin it.*

*One primary reason is the flexibility of money as a form of assistance. A poorer country knows its own shortages better than any outside donor. Funds can be directed at whichever need is most urgent, whereas donated goods arrive in whatever form the donor chose. A government facing a failed harvest can buy food immediately, while a shipment of farm machinery would arrive too late to matter.*

*Nonetheless, money alone leaves the receiving country dependent on the next payment. Cash is spent once and then gone. Training and equipment stay in the country and keep producing value long after the donor has withdrawn, which a grant paid out as income cannot do. A clinic staffed by locally trained nurses continues to treat patients after the funding ends, whereas one staffed by visiting foreign doctors stops working the day they leave.*

*That said, the two forms of help are not really rivals. Practical assistance has to be paid for before it can be delivered. Money is what builds the school or the workshop, and expertise is what keeps it running once built. A donor who funds a training college and then supplies its first instructors has used both forms toward a single result.*

*In conclusion, the long term benefits of practical assistance outweigh those of money alone provided that enough funding exists to establish it. Donors who choose one and refuse the other weaken whichever they chose.*
